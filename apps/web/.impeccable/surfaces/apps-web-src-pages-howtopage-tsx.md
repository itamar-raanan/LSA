# How To Surface Brief

- Scope and mode: authenticated `/how-to` console guide in Read mode.
- Audience: first-time security analysts, SecOps operators, administrators, and auditors.
- Job: understand what LSA does, choose offline Ansible or managed-agent collection, follow the normal investigation path, and recognize the current safety boundary.
- Primary action: enter the appropriate operational workspace from a task-oriented guide; administrator-only destinations remain role-aware.
- Proof and content: repository-backed collection behavior, normalized evidence lifecycle, application and vulnerability correlation, fleet policy, evidence retention, and audit-only remediation governance.
- Constraints: preserve the warm Geist console system, avoid duplicate product documentation, keep technical claims current, remain useful in connected and air-gapped deployments, and fit desktop and mobile without horizontal overflow.
- Direction: workflow switchboard. Begin with the common Collect → Validate → Correlate → Investigate → Govern path, compare the two collection methods, then lead into an analyst workflow and capability directory.
- Memorable moment: the two collection paths visibly converge into one evidence model before the analyst workflow begins.
- Unresolved decisions: none for this release; deeper deployment commands remain in the package and evidence workspaces rather than being duplicated here.
